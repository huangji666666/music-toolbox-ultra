OpenScore Lieder corpus excerpts

Source project: https://github.com/OpenScore/Lieder
Source score pages are recorded per exercise in sight-library-v2.js.
Score data license: Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)
License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This application stores short, two-measure sight-singing excerpts generated
from the corpus score data. Stable source IDs and content fingerprints are
included so the local library can be updated without duplicate exercises.

The OpenScore project asks downstream users to credit OpenScore and link to
the project even though CC0 does not legally require attribution. This app
keeps that credit in the local library information and in this notice.
